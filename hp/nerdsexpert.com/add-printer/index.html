HOW TO CONNECT A PRINTER

Add Your Printer in Just a Few Steps

SysNet Cleaner makes it easy to discover and connect compatible printers.
Follow the simple steps below to get your printer ready to use.

01

Open Printers & Scanners

Open the Settings application on your computer and navigate to Bluetooth & Devices. From there, select Printers & Scanners to access printer management options. This section allows you to view installed printers, manage printing preferences, and begin the process of adding a new printer to your system.

02

Click Add Device

In the Printers & Scanners window, locate the Add Device button near the top of the page. Click this option to allow Windows to search for nearby printers and compatible devices. Make sure your printer is powered on and ready for detection before continuing with the setup process.

03

Select Your Printer

Wait for Windows to complete the device search and display available printers. Review the list carefully and select the printer you want to connect. Confirm that the displayed device name matches your printer model before proceeding to ensure the correct printer is added to your computer.

04

Complete the Setup

Follow the on-screen instructions to finish the installation process. Windows may automatically install the required drivers and configure the printer settings. Once setup is complete, your printer will appear in the printer list and be ready for printing, scanning, and other supported tasks.
