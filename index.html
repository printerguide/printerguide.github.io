Add a printer to Windows in four steps

Power the printer on and connect it to the same network as your PC (or plug in USB), then follow these steps.

  1. Open Printers & scannersSettings, then Bluetooth & devices, then Printers & scanners.
  2. Select Add deviceWindows searches for nearby printers. Keep the printer on and close by.
  3. Pick your printerChoose the entry that matches your model name from the list.
  4. Finish and testLet Windows install the driver, then print a test page to confirm.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you start.

Do I need to pay for any of this?

No. Every guide on DailyTech Guides is free to read and follow.

Why can't my printer see my Wi-Fi network?

Many printers only support 2.4 GHz networks. Move the printer closer to the router, check the network band, and restart both devices.

Where should I download printer drivers?

Only from the website of your printer's manufacturer. Search for your exact model number plus "drivers" and check the address belongs to the brand.

Is it safe to let someone remotely control my PC to fix a printer?

Be very careful. Genuine printer setup rarely needs remote access. Never give remote access, card details or passwords to anyone who contacted you first.

What if these steps do not fix it?

Contact your printer maker's official support with your model and serial number. They can also confirm warranty cover.

Which printer should I buy?

For occasional printing, a compact inkjet is fine. For high volume, an ink-tank or laser printer usually costs less per page.
